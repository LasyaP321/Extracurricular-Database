The Yale Young Global Scholars Program is a summer academic program for high school students interested in global issues, leadership, and interdisciplinary study. Participants engage in rigorous coursework, collaborate with peers from around the world, and learn from Yale faculty and industry leaders. This program fosters critical thinking and inspires students to become informed global citizens and leaders.

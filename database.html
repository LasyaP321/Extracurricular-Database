MIT THINK Scholars Program

Research Remote Free 2 Months Advanced Science Research 9th 10th 11th 12th

The MIT THINK Scholars Program invites high school students passionate about STEM to propose innovative projects addressing real-world problems, focusing on creativity, originality, and impact. Selected students receive mentorship from MIT students, up to $1,000 in project funding, and a fully funded trip to MIT to present their work. THINK provides valuable resources, including guidance on research methods and access to MIT’s extensive network, empowering young researchers to make meaningful contributions in STEM.
